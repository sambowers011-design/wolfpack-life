Terms

The short version.

Wolfpack Life is an independent student demo project, built to explore what a "your week, one board" app for NC State students could look like. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by NC State University.

No warranty

This is a demo. It's provided as-is, with no guarantee it will keep working, keep your data, or be available tomorrow. There's no support team behind it.

Your data can disappear

Everything you enter lives in your browser's storage, not on a server. Clearing your browser data, switching browsers, or switching devices will lose it. See the privacy page for the full breakdown.

Use it like a demo

Don't enter a real password you use elsewhere, and don't rely on this for anything that actually matters — assignment deadlines, registration dates, or anything with real stakes.

Changes

Since this is a small, evolving project, features and pages may change, break, or disappear without notice.

Questions about NC State's actual official services should go to NC State directly — this project has no connection to them.
