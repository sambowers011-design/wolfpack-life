Privacy

Where your data actually goes.

Wolfpack Life is an independent student demo project with no backend server and no database. Here's exactly what that means for your data:

Accounts

When you create an account, your name, email, and a hashed version of your password are saved to your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere — there's no server to send it to.

Classes, tasks, and sessions

Your class schedule and tasks are stored the same way, in your browser's localStorage, tied to the account you signed in with. Your current sign-in session lives in sessionStorage and clears when you close the tab.

Guest mode

Anything you add while browsing as a guest lives only in sessionStorage for that tab. Close the tab and it's gone.

What we don't do

No analytics, no tracking cookies, no third-party scripts reading your data, no data leaving your browser. We can't see what you enter because there's nowhere for it to go.

One honest caveat

Passwords are hashed before storage, but this is still not production-grade security — anyone with access to this browser profile could inspect the stored data. Don't reuse a real password here.
